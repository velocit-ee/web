the problem

small orgs deserve solid infrastructure. they just can't afford to maintain it.

[01]

bus factor of one

One person built it, understands it, maintains it. When they leave — graduation, another job, retirement — the knowledge walks out with them.

[02]

enterprise tools, enterprise price

The platforms that exist were built for Comcast, not a school or community ISP. Small orgs don't have $50k/yr infrastructure budgets.

[03]

fragile by default

Manually configured systems have no memory. When hardware dies or a rebuild is needed, there's no source of truth — just undocumented configs and someone's fading memory.

[04]

connectivity deserts

Community networks and rural ISPs need real tooling to scale affordable internet access. The software to run them barely exists at a price they can afford.

how it works

describe your hardware. get your config. run one command.

AI-assisted setup — answer questions about your gear and use case. velocit.ee generates a complete deployment config. no prior infra expertise required.

infrastructure as code — everything lives in a git repo. ansible, terraform, maas. clone it anywhere, rebuild from scratch, any time.

self-documenting by design — every service, VLAN, and config change is tracked and explained. a new admin with zero prior context can pick up exactly where the last one left off.

self-healing — automatic updates, alerting, and recovery runbooks baked in from the start. not bolted on later.
